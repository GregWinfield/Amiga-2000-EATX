# Component decisions for the Commodore-authentic EATX

The factory parts list distinguishes axial ceramic capacitors, radial electrolytics, discrete axial resistors, bussed SIPs and isolated resistor arrays. Package appearance is useful only after the function and connections are known. The comparisons below refer to Commodore 312721 **Rev 6**, the **6.0 parts list**, the identified **6.2 donor** and Jason Beer's **EATX R3.1**. References attributed to the reconstruction are identified separately. ## Historical parts that need individual treatment

- **SIPs.** The historical register gives every reconstructed RP/RPF physical pin and the two omitted RP900/RP901 options. It records isolated pairs separately from bussed packs. The 6.0 parts list gives RP205 as 120 Ω and RP903/RP908 as 3.3 kΩ. The subsequent redraw, donor-derived BOM and EATX use 1 kΩ at these functions. RP101 is 4.7 kΩ in the 6.0 parts list and reconstructed KiCad, 2.7 kΩ on the redraw and EATX. The Sprint author's correction note supersedes the older text BOM at this reference. None of these apparent revision changes authorises changing an EATX value. [Historical network inventory](audit/rev62/historical-networks.csv) · [Redraw](https://www.amigawiki.org/dnl/schematics/A2000_R6.pdf)
- **Discretes.** Factory sheet 6 distinguishes individual grounded RC returns R604/R605 from similarly named packs. In other circuits, R307 is 68 Ω, R308 is 200 Ω and R310 is a 47 Ω higher-power part. The usual factory resistor listing is 5%, ¼ W with explicit exceptions. Inspect voltage, dissipation and lead form separately. The reconstruction often uses a 10.16 mm axial pitch, including positions for which B+ uses a compact body. [Factory parts list, printed pp. 1–2](https://amiga.net.au/files/Tech_Amiga/Commodore_System_Schematics_A2000_Revision_6_ocr.pdf)
- **Capacitors.** The factory lists the 1 nF terminations and many 0.1 µF bypasses as axial MLC, the DRAM 0.33 µF group as radial MLC, and 4.7 µF C224/C415/C811 as radial electrolytics. Use radial ceramics for short B+ terminations and selected capacitors where drill space favours them. Confirm capacitance at bias for class-II dielectrics, and resistance, ripple and polarity for electrolytics. [Factory parts list](https://amiga.net.au/files/Tech_Amiga/Commodore_System_Schematics_A2000_Revision_6_ocr.pdf)
- **ICs.** Large socketed parts and DIP logic dominate the factory placement. Historical U600–605 use different LS/ALS bus drivers, and U540/U541 are F244s. An authentic silhouette does not establish timing equivalence to the EATX family. The per-reference conversion register retains the previously identified manufacturer candidates and physical pin checks.
