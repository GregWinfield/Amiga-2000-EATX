# Right-channel audio shunt

R234 brings the board to 225 THT and 329 SMD. The right-channel output retains its 390 Ω shunt from FLTRIGHT to GND. R234 uses the same Vishay MRS16000C3900FCT00 as the left-channel R244. The upright 2.54 mm footprint and the new C306 ground landing together provide a local return behind the audio connectors. The FLTRIGHT branch adds 0.955 mm. A separate 0.381 mm bottom GND track runs 2.992 mm to C306.2. The R234 ground pad has four front thermal paths, four inner paths and two bottom paths. All five changed outer ground contours have plated contacts. [Copper lengths](routing.csv) · [Ground sections](spokes.json) · [Contacts](ground-contacts.json)

The selected axial metal-film resistor is 390 Ω ±1%, 0.4 W at 70 °C, with a maximum working voltage of 200 V subject to the power limit, and a −55 to +155 °C operating range. The maximum body is 3.6 × 1.6 mm with nominal 0.50 mm leads. Mouser listed the exact ordering code in stock. Form the free return above the body, allow clearance from the rear hybrid and inspect C234 before fitting R234 and C306. The final courtyards are 0.332 mm apart at C306, 0.620 mm at C234 and 1.283 mm at HY200. Check lead-form access and compare loaded audio response and noise on both channels. The final refill shows reference changes only at the local FLTRIGHT junction and its ground route. All new copper has plane reference outside same-net terminal regions. Every schematic pin, pad-net assignment, fixed datum and discrete via agrees with the previous batch. ERC, DRC, parity and opens reconcile individually. This batch also updates the visible R310 value to 47 Ω, 1 W in both CAD files and the installed BOM.
