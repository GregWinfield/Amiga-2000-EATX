# XCLK and video-slot clock series resistors

R212 retains 22 Ω between U303.3 (74HCT08) and the XCLK input at U109.14 (74F258). Its source-side copper increases from 8.556 to 10.099 mm and its XCLK side decreases from 148.254 to 143.973 mm. R905 retains 47 Ω on the Buster U800.22 / video-slot CN203.19 clock path. Its Buster-side net shortens by 0.098 mm and its _C4 side by 0.584 mm. C913 and C920 retain their existing clock loads, pad assignments and copper beyond the local endpoints. The only changed trace objects are the listed terminal connections. Both horizontal DIN0204 parts use the Vishay MBA0204 HF non-helically trimmed film family, ±1%, on 5.08 mm centres. The original 1206 ordering codes and tolerances are unspecified. Allow 3.6 × 1.6 mm for a maximum body and form the 0.5 mm nominal leads without bearing on the end caps. The nominal finished drill is 0.70 mm. R212 has 1.016 mm courtyard clearance to R902 and R905 has 0.467 mm to C913. Check seating and hot-tool access with the adjacent clock capacitors populated.
