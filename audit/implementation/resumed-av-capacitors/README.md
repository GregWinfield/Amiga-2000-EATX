# Video reservoir and audio coupling capacitors

C214 and C226 retain 470 µF each from +VID to GND after the 1 Ω R215 feed. Panasonic EEU-FR1C471 is a 16 V, ±20%, −40 to +105 °C aluminium electrolytic, with an 8 × 11.5 mm nominal can, 3.5 mm leads, maximum 56 mΩ impedance and 950 mA ripple rating at 100 kHz. The two parallel cans give a nominal 169 Hz feed pole. The original local ceramics remain at their loads. An assumed 20 nH of additional series inductance per can contributes 12.6 mΩ at 100 kHz. This is a sensitivity calculation. The supplier gives no original can MPN for a direct impedance comparison. Both new supply and return branches use 0.70 mm copper. The nearest radial courtyards are 1.18 mm apart. Allow 14 mm above the PCB for the video cans including length tolerance and seating allowance, plus vent clearance before fitting an enclosure. [Panasonic FR dimensions and electrical ratings](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/RDF0000/ABA0000C1259.pdf)

C233 and C243 retain 22 µF coupling from the two U204 buffer outputs, with pin 1 at the buffer. C317 retains its original polarity between AUDIN and CN305 pin 43. The layout reference is Nichicon UKL1E220MDD, 25 V, ±20%, 5 × 11 mm body, 2 mm pitch, −40 to +85 °C and 1.1 µA maximum leakage. The specified 0.08 dissipation factor bounds equivalent series resistance at about 4.8 Ω at 120 Hz and nominal capacitance, compared with the 1 kΩ R233/R243 and 10 kΩ R202 branches. A 20 nH lead-inductance sensitivity contributes 2.5 mΩ at 20 kHz. The 120 Hz value does not establish broadband ESR. Nearest courtyard gaps exceed 2.27 mm. Allow 14 mm vertical assembly space and keep the adjacent jumper serviceable. Nichicon marks this part end of life. Procurement must establish traceable stock or qualify a current low-leakage part for the same footprint before release. Verify DC polarity with the external interface connected, both audio channels under load and hot/cold video-rail startup on hardware. [Nichicon exact part](https://www.nichicon.com/en-us/part/ukl1e220mdd/6428/)

The reference-plane comparison found changes only at the connected video and audio traces near their own new holes. The longest added-route loss of inner ground beyond 1.3 mm from these holes was 0.15 mm on AUDIN. Every sampled original bottom route, all auxiliary power areas and all zone contour counts matched. The five sites lie outside the Zorro socket field. Physical case, cable and vent clearance remain assembly checks.
