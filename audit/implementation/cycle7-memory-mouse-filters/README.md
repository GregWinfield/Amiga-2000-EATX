# Audio, RTC, negative supply, mouse and memory

Five installed substitutions bring the motherboard to 193 through-hole packages. R500-10 keeps 47 Ω on DRAB9 and shortens the private driver by 2.540 mm and the load net by 0.795 mm. RN204B1 keeps 68 Ω on mouse quadrature M1H. Its two leaves gain 2.777 and 3.729 mm. Both use ±1% Vishay non-helical RF film on 5.08 mm centres. All sampled surviving traces retain their adjacent reference. The mouse Zb leaf crosses 0.546 mm outside the 1.4 mm terminal neighbourhood versus 0.537 mm before. Adjacent ground lies within 0.30 mm in each case. Check the loaded mouse thresholds and DRAM edges at the far receiver. [Copper lengths](routing.csv) · [Reference samples](route-return.json) · [Vishay RF specifications](https://www.vishay.com/docs/28769/mbasmahf.pdf)

C244 puts the 100 nF left audio shunt on a 2.248 mm leaf at R244.2. With a low-impedance source, 1 kΩ in parallel with 390 Ω gives an illustrative 28 µs RC time constant. Check both populated audio channels for level and filter response. C812 places its 100 nF CLKVCC bypass on a 4.251 mm connection to U899.18. The 10 GΩ insulation resistance specification corresponds to 0.3 nA at 3 V. Verify retention and assembled board leakage. C413 connects the nonpolar 10 nF, 50 V radial to the existing C412.2 negative supply terminal with 4.211 mm of copper. Check −5 V ripple and transients under load. The minimum courtyard gaps are 0.038 mm from C244 to CN305, 0.260 mm from C812 to U899, 0.122 mm from C413 to C405, 0.078 mm from RN204B1 to RN200 and 0.046 mm from R500-10 to R500-15. Check connector overhang at C244 with sockets fitted, axial lead dressing, tool approach and underside soldering. The capacitors use 2.54 mm lead pitch, 1.60 mm lands and 0.80 mm finished holes. The two frame ends by the mouse bank were shortened and the ALL68 legend moved 0.8 mm for pad clearance.

All 31 changed outer ground contours contact same-net copper. The main inner +5 V polygon retains its plated contacts. Its refill also produces a fifth triangular rounding artifact at the next Zorro socket, identical in area to the four present at the other sockets. Each is 56,400 nm² with a longest dimension of about 0.114 mm and an effective perpendicular width below 1 nm. It carries no useful copper or current. Keep the positive-plane slivers on the fabricator CAM review. Auxiliary rails retain their filled shape. All vias, electrical assignments and mechanical datums are preserved.
