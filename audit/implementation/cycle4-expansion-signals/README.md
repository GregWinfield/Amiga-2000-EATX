The C748 stub uses the existing socket and capacitor terminals on B.Cu. Its bus net gains 2.26 mm. The existing bus trunks and vias remain intact. The longest private branch increase is 5.14 mm. At 0.05 mm sampling, 0.60 mm of the new C748 underside leaf falls outside the adjacent +5 V copper beyond 1.4 mm of a same-net through terminal. Other new traces meet the adjacent reference outside the terminal neighbourhood. Check loaded-slot ringing and receiver margin. R111 retains the 470 Ω pull-up from +5 V to the existing shared bus-error net labelled `_BEER`. Its 1% Vishay MRS25 DIN0207 sits upright. At 5.25 V the worst static resistor dissipation with the line low is below 60 mW. The driven copper is intact and the new tap is 0.757 mm. The longest continuous change to the inner-ground reference under the existing signal is 1.395 mm at its own new termination, with solid ground within 0.60 mm. Verify the bus-error waveform at the processor with representative loads. [Resistor characteristics and package](https://www.vishay.com/docs/28724/mrs16m25.pdf)

C700 is the distributed ISA +5 V bypass. Its 220 nF KEMET C315C224K5R5TA is ±10%, 50 V X7R, rated −55 to +125 °C. The leads are on 2.54 mm centres, within the existing C315 envelope. Its new supply and ground branches are 0.4 and 0.1 mm. Both terminals can reach their respective inner planes directly. All other slot bypasses remain. A series inductance sensitivity of 10–20 nH gives a 2.4–3.4 MHz resonance at nominal capacitance. Effective capacitance at 5 V, lead dressing and the actual impedance spectrum require a populated comparison. The adjacent CN700 B2 trace has a local 0.849 mm reference interruption with ground within 0.45 mm. Measure slot rail bounce and the B2 waveform during card activity.
