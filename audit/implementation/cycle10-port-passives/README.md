**R310** feeds parallel-port pin 14 through D301 and EMI310. The EATX value is 47 Ω, originally marked 0.5 W. The historical tolerance is ±5%. At 10 mA the upper dissipation is 4.94 mW. With the port grounded, 5.25 V and minimum resistance of 44.65 Ω give a conservative continuous 0.6173 W without credit for D301's drop. The selected Vishay PR01000104709JR500 is a 47 Ω ±5%, 1 W at 70 °C, 350 V power metal-film axial resistor. The 1 W device has 0.383 W of margin at rated ambient under that fault assumption. As an additional design limit, linear derating from 1 W at 70 °C to zero at 155 °C leaves 0.824 W at 85 °C and meets 0.6173 W through about 102.5 °C. The datasheet gives 135 K/W under its test conditions, corresponding to roughly 83 K rise at this load. Actual board and lead temperatures depend on the mounting. Check the grounded-port load, body, solder joints, adjacent passives and connector in a populated enclosure.
