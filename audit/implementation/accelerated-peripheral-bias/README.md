# Floppy, LED and audio-control resistors

R305 ties the Q300 base/clamp junction to ground with 10 kΩ. It now has a 0.483 mm connection to the junction and a ground thermal. R300 biases the motor-control transistor. R902 drives the Q302 base, R308 limits LED current, and R214 connects _CUTOFF to the Q202 base. Pin assignments and values are in the conversion ledger.

R305 and the existing R306 use MRS16 bodies, each at most 3.6 mm long and 1.6 mm across. Their parallel axes are separated by 2.594 mm where the bodies overlap. This leaves 0.994 mm between centred maximum-size bodies. For a 0.75 mm finished bore and the nominal 0.50 mm lead, each part can shift 0.125 mm laterally in the hole. Allowing another 0.20 mm of forming error per part leaves 0.344 mm of illustrative body clearance. These are assembly assumptions. The supplier specifies nominal lead diameter. Confirm forming tolerance, lead size and finished holes on samples. Fit nearby SMD first, form both resistors to the 5.08 mm grid, solder from below and inspect the D300 side before fitting larger components. [Vishay dimensions, page 3](https://www.vishay.com/docs/28724/mrs16m25.pdf)

The original 0.080 mm courtyard gap was between the old R305 and R306. The new courtyard and true nearest neighbours are in the verification file. R305 introduces a local ground-plane clearance below an existing _DKRST trace and the base/clamp junction. R214 locally affects its two existing control traces. The affected traces retain their layers, geometry and continuity. No sampled preserved back trace changes reference. All added front segments retain underlying ground outside their own pad neighbourhoods. Auxiliary power copper and contour counts match. Three assembly values moved to the fabrication drawing on their own side. Loaded reset, filter control and indicator behaviour remain physical test items.
