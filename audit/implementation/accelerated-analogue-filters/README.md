# Bipolar analogue supply filters

C222 and C223 are both 47 µF. C222 pin 1 is +AV and pin 2 is AUDIO. C223 pin 1 is AUDIO and pin 2 is −AV. Both use Panasonic EEU-FR1E470 aluminium electrolytics, 25 V, ±20%, −40 to +105 °C, nominal 5 × 11 mm can and 2 mm pin pitch. Panasonic specifies 0.300 Ω maximum impedance at 100 kHz, 280 mA ripple at 105 °C and 11.7 µA maximum leakage. Allow the manufacturer's dimensional tolerances and lead standoff when checking vertical clearance. Both locations are in the analogue section outside the Zorro sockets. Preserve vent clearance and polarity. [Panasonic specification](https://na.industrial.panasonic.com/products/capacitors/aluminum-electrolytic-capacitors/lineup/aluminum-electrolytic-capacitors-radial-lead-type/series/83367/model/83711) · [FR dimensional drawing](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/RDF0000/ABA0000C1259.pdf)

Each rail is fed through 10 Ω. Nominal RC cutoff is 339 Hz, or roughly 282–423 Hz over ±20% capacitance. At 100 kHz the specified impedance is at most 3% of the series feed resistance. Worst specified leakage causes at most 117 µV across 10 Ω. The original parallel 0.22 µF and 0.1 µF ceramics and their routes are retained on each rail. At 100 kHz an added lead inductance of 20 nH would contribute 0.013 Ω. This is a sensitivity assumption. The original shopping list gives no MPN for the 8 × 10 mm cans. ESR and ESL over frequency, rail ripple, loaded stereo noise and hot/cold startup still need comparison on hardware.

The local 0.381 mm AUDIO bridge at C223 restores the two original trace ends previously joined by the broad SMD pad. The remaining added branches use 0.70 mm tracks. Only the corresponding +AV and −AV front tracks change their sampled ground reference around their own new leads. Back-route samples, auxiliary rail areas and contour counts match. No other bulk capacitor is included in this batch.
