# Inner +5 V socket-pour clipping

The saved KiCad filled polygons contain five skinny triangles on In2.Cu, one at each CN601–CN605. The low-priority main +5 V zone (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000061f11a4a`) meets the higher-priority socket-row +5 V pour at each location. Each triangle has a nonzero area of 56,400 nm², a long dimension of about 0.114 mm and a perpendicular height of 0.986202 nm. At CN601 the integer-nanometre vertices are (192272455, 227087765), (192270637, 227093357) and (192305982, 226984577). The first vertex is 0.140 nm from the higher-priority filled edge joining (192251134, 227153384) and (192319622, 226942602). Single-layer diagnostic Gerber exports of the opening and final boards reproduced the geometry. At 4.6 metric precision all five are separate positive G36 regions sharing a vertex with a larger +5 V region. At 4.5 precision three thin regions fall inside a larger positive region. For the other two, the centroid and two vertices lie in or on a larger positive region. The remaining vertex touches the main fill. Neither precision gives a fabricated width on which electrical continuity can depend. No required connection relies on these triangles. Their treatment during flattening and feature-size filtering remains a manufacturing question.
