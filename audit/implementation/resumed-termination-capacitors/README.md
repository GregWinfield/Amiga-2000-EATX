# Six radial termination capacitors

C664 and C672 retain separate BD0 and BD4 RC branches. C711, C776, C789 and C794 retain their individual ISA slot taps and resistor-network pins. Each original 1 nF capacitor is replaced by KEMET C315C102J5G5TA, C0G, ±5%, 50 V, −55 to +125 °C. The maximum body is 3.81 × 2.54 mm. Budget 4.66 mm above the PCB including the coating at the leads. The project footprint has a maximum-body fabrication outline, a visible polarity-independent outline on silk and 0.80 mm finished holes on 2.54 mm pitch. A 0.61 mm maximum lead leaves 0.19 mm nominal diametral play. The 1.60 mm pads leave 0.40 mm nominal annular rings. Assembly still requires the established finished-hole and registration controls. [KEMET component drawing and electrical data](https://www.ariat-tech.pl/datasheet/62/C315C102J5G5TA.pdf)

Each capacitor is in series with its own 1 kΩ return. Nominal RC time constant is 1 µs. Rated dissipation factor of 0.1% at 1 MHz gives about 0.16 Ω equivalent series resistance there. An assumed 20 nH incremental loop inductance adds 12.6 Ω reactance at 100 MHz, compared with 1 kΩ series resistance. The longest added bus-side branch is 1.60 mm, about 11 ps one way at 7 ps/mm. These bounds support layout evaluation. Direct pin capacitance, high-frequency ringing, card loading and connector insertion clearance still require representative hardware.

Original track segments, vias and socket positions match the control. Changed reference samples on preserved front traces are confined to the connected RC branches at their own leads. All sampled back routes retain their references. All added traces retain adjacent ground beyond the immediate 1.3 mm lead neighbourhood. Every auxiliary rail area and contour count matches the control. R605's reference was moved for legibility. C619 stays SMD: its trial removed 6.3101 mm² of +12 V copper. The implemented board excludes those two drill sites.
