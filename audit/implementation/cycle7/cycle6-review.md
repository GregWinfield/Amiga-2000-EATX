# Cycle 6 damping and populated envelopes

The schematic and original shopping list identify twelve 47 Ω, 1206 series resistors. The shopping list leaves the 47 Ω SMD ordering code blank. Original individual tolerance, film construction and package parasitics are undocumented. All twelve selected Vishay MBA0204AC4709FCT00 resistors are non-helically trimmed RF film, 47 Ω ±1% (46.53–47.47 Ω), with 0.25 W long-term rating at 70 °C and a 5.0 mm minimum mounting span. They are horizontal on 5.08 mm centres. Vishay supplies frequency-dependent impedance curves. Actual driver, assembly and card loads still require measurement. All 24 route ends shortened. The video changes remove 1.078–1.140 mm per end. The memory driver changes remove 1.107–1.140 mm and the load sides 1.138–1.140 mm. The copper comparison identifies the shortened leaves, preserved taps and vias, and zero opens, new dangling ends or short findings. Video retained the sampled adjacent reference. The longest contiguous memory reference change was 0.924 mm near its own terminal, with ground within 0.95 mm. At an assumed 7 ps/mm a 1.14 mm trace reduction is about 8 ps one way. A hypothetical additional 1 pF at 47 Ω gives 47 ps. Neither number bounds ringing on a loaded board. Check overshoot, monotonic threshold crossings and DRAM timing at the farthest populated receiver and at the video slot with representative cards.
