# ATX soft-power switch Q9100

The onsemi 2N3904G replaces the MMBT3904 in the low-side PS-ON switch. Both devices are 40 V, 200 mA NPN transistors. Both specify a maximum 0.2 V saturation voltage at 10 mA collector and 1 mA base current, 4 pF maximum output capacitance, and minimum gain of 100 at 10 mA in the linear-region test. The existing U9102 latch drives the base through R9104, 4.7 kΩ. At 5 V drive and 0.8 V base-emitter drop this supplies about 0.89 mA. The transistor switches a PSU logic input. Verify asserted and released PS-ON voltages with the intended supply, including standby startup and repeated power cycles. [Original SOT-23 ratings](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/mmbt3904lt1-d.pdf) · [onsemi TO-92 package and electrical ratings](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/2n3903-d.pdf)

Viewed as marked on the silkscreen, physical leads 1, 2 and 3 are emitter, base and collector. They correspond to PCB pads 2, 1 and 3. Pad 2 grounds the emitter directly through three sampled inner-plane spokes. The retired 2.116 mm emitter spur and its ground via served only this transistor. The collector remains on the original active-low PSU input and the base remains on R9104. The package moved from the underside to the component side. The closest courtyard is R308 at 0.308 mm. Allow the manufacturer's 5.20 mm body height plus lead-form standoff. Verify assembled height, underside trim and pin forming with a sample. The 0.90 mm holes, 1.45 mm lands, annular-ring tolerance budget and maximum lead diagonal are identical to the indicator driver. [Lead and finished-hole tolerance budget](../resumed-indicator-driver/README.md)

Both replacement branches stay on B.Cu. The base branch grows from 6.869 to 11.589 mm, and the collector from 26.952 to 28.584 mm. At 0.05 mm sample spacing, collector length outside the adjacent +5 V plane grows from 2.044 to 3.215 mm. All 1.146 mm of missing adjacent +5 V on the new base branch is within 1.3 mm of the new terminals. The worst additional propagation on either branch is about 33 ps at 7 ps/mm. These are latched power-control signals. The new emitter connects directly to the ground plane in both standby and operation. No preserved route in the sampled hole neighbourhood changed its reference. Every inner-zone contour count and every auxiliary rail area matched. Native ERC, DRC, parity and connectivity comparisons introduced no new finding.
