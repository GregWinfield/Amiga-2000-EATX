# Audio and video supply feeds

R200 and R215 remain 1 Ω series feeds to +AUD and +VID, and R208 remains the 10 Ω feed from −12 V to −AV. The new power tracks are 0.70 mm on the 1 Ω circuits and 0.4572 mm on R208. No new via is in series with any feed. With 35 µm copper at 20 °C, the added track resistance is approximately 5.2 mΩ for R200, 6.8 mΩ for R215 and 3.9 mΩ for R208. These estimates exclude plating, temperature rise and existing copper. The only changed reference samples on a preserved route are three at the +VID lead. The inner auxiliary copper areas and plane contour counts match.

The selected straight-lead PR01 1 Ω, ±1%, 1 W parts fit 10.16 mm centres with a maximum body of 6.5 × 2.5 mm and 0.58 ±0.05 mm leads. R208 uses the MBA 0204 10 Ω, ±1%, 0.4 W part with a 3.6 × 1.6 mm maximum body and nominal 0.50 mm leads. The changed part families supply published pulse curves.

The netlist places 470.1 µF on +AUD, 940.3 µF on +VID and 47.32 µF on −AV. As a startup screen, assume initially discharged capacitors, +20% capacitance, minimum resistor tolerance for peak power, +5.25 V and −13.2 V. Ideal step peaks are 27.9 W on each 1 Ω feed and 17.6 W on the 10 Ω feed. The equal-energy rectangular widths, conservatively calculated with maximum resistance, are 0.285 ms, 0.570 ms and 0.287 ms respectively. These fall below the PR01 low-duty curve at a repetition period of at least one second and the MBA single-pulse curve. External accessory load and repeated hot cycling are separate from capacitor charging. Record steady voltage drop, actual surge and body temperature at cold start and maximum intended load before hardware qualification.
