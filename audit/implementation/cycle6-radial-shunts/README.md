# Comparator reference and Zorro grant termination capacitors

**C815** retains the 100 nF shunt on the LM339 reference. The KEMET C315C104K5R5TA is nonpolar, ±10%, 50 V X7R, with 10 GΩ specified insulation resistance. At 5.25 V that resistance corresponds to 0.53 nA. RN802 has a 1 kΩ leg to each rail, giving a nominal parallel resistance of at most 500 Ω with the other branches connected. The corresponding first-order scale is at most 50 µs at nominal capacitance. Temperature, DC bias, aging and the actual comparator inputs affect the populated transient. Confirm reset and keyboard thresholds during power-up and supply disturbances. [KEMET part drawing and electrical specifications](https://docs.rs-online.com/aa05/0900766b81708ddf.pdf)

The original 10.936 mm ground spur ran back to RN802.3. The new ground terminal connects directly to In1 and the two outer pours. The 4.603 mm B.Cu reference tap starts at the plated RN802.5 terminal. The original divider-to-comparator path is continuous across the former SMD land. The capacitor clears RN802 by 1.060 mm. An earlier placement had only one bottom thermal spoke and disturbed the reference under D15. Moving the capacitor to the divider removed both findings.

**C675** retains the independent 1 nF, 50 V, ±5% C0G KEMET C315C102J5G5TA in the CN601 grant termination. The socket-side _GBG copper shortened by 0.125 mm. The private connection to the 1 kΩ R606 grew by 0.287 mm to 3.057 mm. The 1 µs nominal RC time constant and fixed socket tap are preserved. The established 20 nH sensitivity adds 12.6 Ω at 100 MHz against the 1 kΩ series element. Confirm the grant edge with realistic card loads. [C315 termination qualification](../resumed-termination-capacitors/README.md)

Both footprints accept 0.61 mm maximum leads in 0.80 mm holes on 2.54 mm centres. Allow 3.81 × 2.54 mm for the body and 4.66 mm above the board including lead coating. The C675 courtyard clears R606 by 0.121 mm and the CN601 courtyard by 2.070 mm. Check the fitted card overhang, lead dressing and soldering access.

All inner zone outline counts and auxiliary rail areas matched the control. Outer GND gained three front contours and one back contour. Each changed output polygon intersects a plated GND land or via. C815 has four spokes to the inner ground plane. Two front sectors and one back sector terminate in small copper lobes, each bonded to C815.2. Two spokes on each outer layer reach larger pours. No new isolated copper or thermal diagnostic appeared. All new routes have their adjacent reference outside 1.4 mm of their same-net through terminals. The sampled reference change on a preserved route is confined to a GND trace at the new ground land.
