# Five video-series resistors

RN201A1 through RN201D1 keep their 47 Ω series connections on the B0, B3, G3 and R3 branches to CN203. RN203E1 keeps 47 Ω between U205 and PIXELSW. Each horizontal DIN0204 occupies the original resistor centre. Both lands lie on the original straight trace. Each side loses 1.078–1.140 mm of copper. The ten original trace ends were shortened at their free ends, preserving the external connector, taps, vias and other routes. The widened “ALL 47” legend and relocated CN203 reference clear the new lands.

The selected Vishay MBA0204AC4709FCT00 is a 47 Ω ±1% non-helically trimmed RF resistor. Its 3.6 × 1.6 mm maximum body, 0.5 mm nominal wire and 5.0 mm minimum mounting span fit the 5.08 mm horizontal footprint with 0.70 mm holes. The minimum courtyard gap is 1.112 mm. CN203 lacks a courtyard. Its footprint silk reaches approximately x = 213.8 mm on the resistor side, leaving over 2.5 mm to the new courtyards. Confirm the connector and card envelope when populated. [Manufacturer drawing, frequency curves and ordering code](https://www.vishay.com/docs/28769/mbasmahf.pdf)

The resistor is nonpolar. Its long-term 70 °C rating is 250 mW, corresponding to 73 mA RMS in 47 Ω. These are series digital loads. As a sensitivity check, 100 pF charged and discharged through a full 5.25 V swing at 30 MHz draws 83 mW per line from the supply, only part of which heats the resistor. An additional 1 pF at a pad gives a 47 ps RC scale with 47 Ω. These are assumed loads for comparison. Confirm edge crossings, overshoot and dissipation with the actual card and driver. The new lands introduce a barrel discontinuity. Their short horizontal lead form and the manufacturer's RF impedance curves are the basis for reopening these formerly retained positions.

The refill changed no sampled reference below retained front or back routes. The ten shortened traces retain ground outside the 1.3 mm terminal neighbourhood. Every zone retained its contour count. The ten signal leads have plane clearances. All 3,603 schematic assignments and 3,698 pad-net records match. The native DRC lists different examples at its library-mismatch reporting limit after the five original SMD footprints depart. The remaining ERC differences select alternate wire anchors for the same inherited findings.
