# Narrow DIP placement and device comparison

All 32 audited IC positions retained their SOICs. The screen used the published motherboard at the start of Cycle 5. It checked four cardinal orientations, 7.62 mm between rows and 2.54 mm lead pitch, with a 0.254 mm grid in a local 40.64 mm window. Each candidate could retire its own exclusive outer-layer escape to a branch, pad or via within 15 mm. Other board traces, pads, vias and front courtyards remained obstacles. The early pin screen used a circular 1.60 mm land and 0.20 mm foreign-track clearance. The final oval/rectangular lands, filled planes, socket height and new escape routes would require native KiCad checks at any surviving location. No tested position cleared all its holes. Grid totals count orientations and positions, including overlapping alternatives. They do not count distinct installable sockets. [Results for every reference](dip-screen.csv)

| Device | Electrical comparison and physical result |
|---|---|
| U304 | The shopping list specifies TI SN75188DR. SN75188N is its active-production 14-pin PDIP. Both use the same top-view numbering, negative supply on 1, ground on 7 and positive supply on 14. The board uses ±12 V, within the specified ±7.5 to ±15 V supply range subject to temperature derating. The 1.9 V high and 0.8 V low input limits and typical 10 mA output current limit apply to both. At ±9 V, 25 °C, 3 kΩ and 15 pF the maximum propagation delays are 350 and 175 ns. D and N junction-to-ambient thermal impedances are 86 and 80 °C/W under the stated test conditions. The layout screen found zero body-clear sites. [TI driver data](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn75188.pdf) |
| U305 | The shopping list specifies TI SN75189DR. SN75189N is active-production and preserves the 14-pin numbering, ground on 7, +5 V on 14 and four response-control pins left open on this board. Both operate from 4.5–5.5 V. The commercial non-A receiver has rising thresholds of 0.9–1.6 V and falling thresholds of 0.65–1.25 V with controls open. It sinks 10 mA at 0.45 V maximum and sources 0.5 mA at 2.6 V minimum. At 5 V, 25 °C and 15 pF the maximum low-to-high delay is 85 ns with 3.9 kΩ and high-to-low is 50 ns with 390 Ω. At 70 °C TI rates D at 608 mW and N at 736 mW. These asynchronous serial parts have no clock setup/hold requirement. The best body-clear site accommodated two of 14 pins. [TI receiver data](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn75189.pdf) |

The source shopping list identifies only a family and package for the other 30 positions. U203, the 74LS38 open-collector driver, came closest at 12 of 14 pins. A wider design study would need the actual populated device, its pull-up loads, sink current and rise time, and permission to move the copper obstructing the remaining two pins. For the HCT and AHCT bus devices, the remaining checks include output enable/disable, loaded edge rate and timing at the connector. The latches and flip-flops also need setup and hold margins. The LF347 and LM339 need analogue swing, slew, input range and output-stage checks. The per-reference table records the missing evidence. Socket procurement and complete electrical qualification of these 30 positions remain open. [Original shopping list](../../../BOM/A2000EATX-REV31-ShoppingList.csv)
