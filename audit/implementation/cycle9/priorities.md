# Cycle 9 priority decisions

### R310 parallel-port pull-up

R310 feeds pin 14 of the lower parallel connector from +5 V through D301 and EMI310. The native netlist gives D301 cathode to R310.2, R310.1 to EMI310.2, and EMI310.1 to CN305.14. Both original copper exits are 0.4572 mm wide. The EATX schematic and shopping item 116 specify 47 Ω, 0.5 W, 1206, without fitted manufacturer code or tolerance. The Commodore Rev. 6 parts list identifies 901600-15 at R310 and gives a general 5% resistor tolerance. The technical reference budgets 10 mA at the parallel port and allows for a printer grounding this line. The Commodore tolerance supplies a historical design target. The fitted EATX resistor remains unidentified. [Commodore Rev. 6 parts list](https://amiga.net.au/files/Tech_Amiga/Commodore_System_Schematics_A2000_Revision_6.pdf) · [Commodore technical reference](https://support.retrorewind.ca/media/commodore/amiga/commodore_a500-a2000_technical_reference_manual.pdf)

At 10 mA, 47 Ω ±5% drops 0.447–0.494 V and dissipates 4.47–4.94 mW. Grounded pin 14 determines the thermal requirement. Assuming +5 V can reach 5.25 V, the minimum resistance is 44.65 Ω. With no credit for diode drop the upper bound is 118 mA and 0.617 W. A 0.5 W rating would require at least 0.525 V of guaranteed series drop at the limiting condition before ambient derating. D301's design datasheet supplies a maximum forward drop. Its typical curve cannot establish that hot minimum. The bound describes the possible load on the replacement. Actual resistor temperature and diode drop have not been measured. [Design rectifier data](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/S1MWF.pdf)

The Yageo FMP-50JB-52-47R is a catalogue 47 Ω ±5%, 0.5 W axial metal-film part. Its 3.7 × 2.1 mm maximum body and 0.45 ±0.05 mm leads fit the seven screened 7.62 mm layouts. At maximum body length the horizontal form leaves about 1.96 mm on each end for the bend. The continuous grounded-port bound exceeds its rating. Distributor listing establishes the ordering code only. The stocked Vishay PR01000104709JR500 is 47 Ω ±5%, 1 W, 6.5 × 2.5 mm maximum, with 0.58 ±0.05 mm axial wire. Its R5 code specifies axial tape pitch. The checked 10.16 mm horizontal envelope and the compact upright envelope had no qualified two-exit placement. The former short-pitch MBB special remains subject to the preceding procurement hold. Keep R310 SMD. Reopen with a documented short-pitch power-rated device and thermal envelope, or grounded-pin voltage and temperature measurements that bound the compact part. [Yageo FMP dimensions](https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2025/02/2499152.pdf) · [Yageo ordering code](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/yageo/FMP-50JB-52-47R/9115020) · [Vishay package and derating](https://www.vishay.com/docs/28729/pr010203.pdf) · [Vishay sourcing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/vishay-beyschlag-draloric-bc-components/PR01000104709JR500/596060)

### C501 and C600

Both are 100 nF +5 V logic bypasses. Shopping item 17 identifies a ceramic 1206 without a tolerance, voltage rating, dielectric class or fitted ordering code. KEMET C315C104K5R5TA is a stocked 100 nF, ±10%, 50 V X7R candidate. Even a sound layout would leave the exact original tolerance and dielectric to establish. [Manufacturer drawing](https://search.kemet.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/C315C104K5R5TA)

C501's new trial maintained two copper branches to the original distribution entries. The refilled ground pad had two effective front paths and four inner paths. Only one bottom path remained continuous from radius 0.78 to 1.38 mm. The native DRC count alone did not detect this failure of the return-path criterion. Preserve the SMD component and the original hold.

For C600 an isolated refill added a separate 0.80 mm GND stitching via with a 0.40 mm drill at (213.09829, 255.39250) mm. The candidate top copper had 1.312 mm minimum edge-to-centre spacing at the stitch site. The 2.70 mm device-side supply branch terminated at U600.20. KiCad still identified both bottom thermal spokes of C600 as connected to an isolated island. The simultaneous adjacent C601 trial also overlapped C600's courtyard. C600 remains SMD. Any future change must demonstrate two independent connections into grounded outer copper and clearance to the neighbouring buffer bypass. [Trial diagnostics and actual ground sections](trials.json)
